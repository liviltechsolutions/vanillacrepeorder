Treatments

Four ways we fix and finish brows, eyes & lips

Every booking starts with a consultation — we assess your existing pigment and skin before recommending a treatment path.

Signature

Brow & Eyeliner Wash

Our non‑invasive removal technique lifts old, faded or botched pigment out of the skin — no needles, no scarring. The first of its kind in Malaysia.

Correction

Red / Blue / Grey Rescue

Outdated tattoo ink oxidises into tones nature never intended. We neutralise discoloured brows before any new pigment goes in.

Repair

Semi‑Permanent Brow Repair

Uneven, over‑tattooed or asymmetric brows reshaped and refilled with soft, natural strokes suited to your face.

Colour

Lip Blush Colour

A semi‑permanent tint that restores natural rosiness and definition to the lip line.
